\section*{Supplementary Methods}

This file summarizes supporting material for the TSRClust manuscript. The full
replication-level simulation exports, design manifests, real-data outputs, and
analysis scripts are supplied separately in the reproducibility bundle rather
than reproduced as large tables in this PDF.

\section*{S1. Complete Simulation Design}

The simulation design is defined by three manifest groups: architecture,
stress, and negative control. The architecture study used 80 cells and 35,200
generated datasets; the stress study used 30 cells and 9,000 generated
datasets; and the expanded negative-control study used 10 cells and 10,000
generated datasets. The design manifests are provided in
\texttt{results/simulation\_design/}.

\section*{S2. Architecture Cell-Level Results}

Architecture cell summaries are provided in the file
\texttt{architecture\_cell\_summary.csv} under
\texttt{results/summary\_results/}. These
summaries report method-level performance within each design cell and support
the pooled architecture results in the main text.

\section*{S3. Stress Cell-Level Results}

Stress cell summaries are provided in \texttt{stress\_cell\_summary.csv} under
\texttt{results/summary\_results/}. These summaries
identify the one-factor departures under which minority recovery is retained or
lost, including high dimension, weak separation, heavy-tailed macro
populations, diffuse minorities, adjacent minorities, and alternative residual
noise mechanisms.

\section*{S4. Negative-Control Results}

Negative-control summaries are provided in
\texttt{negative\_control\_cell\_summary.csv} and
\texttt{negative\_control\_pooled.csv} under
\texttt{results/summary\_results/}. In the pooled
expanded negative control, TSRClust produced 3 false micro-discoveries in
10,000 successful evaluations, giving an estimated probability of 0.0003 and an
exact 95\% binomial confidence interval of [0.00006, 0.00088]. TSRClust without
validation produced a false micro-discovery in every negative-control run.

\section*{S5. Paired Comparisons And Validation Ablation}

Paired method comparisons against TSRClust are provided for the
architecture, stress, and negative-control studies in the corresponding
\texttt{*\_paired\_vs\_TSRvalidated.csv} files. These files report paired
differences and Monte Carlo standard errors.

\section*{S6. Monte Carlo Uncertainty}

Monte Carlo uncertainty for the false micro-discovery estimate is summarized by
the exact binomial interval above. For other simulation metrics, uncertainty is
reported through cell-level summaries and paired-comparison standard errors in
the reproducibility result files.

\section*{S7. OTRIMLE Sensitivity Analysis}

OTRIMLE was included as an improper-likelihood noise-component competitor. The
main manuscript reports the fixed-\(K\) OTRIMLE results used in the headline
comparison. Sensitivity outputs are included in the reproducibility result
folders so that the fixed-\(K\) choice can be inspected without expanding the
main display budget.

\section*{S8. Detailed Runtime Summaries}

Runtime summaries are provided in the architecture, stress, negative-control,
and real-data summary files. Reported end-to-end runtime includes preprocessing
and every stage needed to obtain final labels.

\section*{S9. Real-Data Trimming Rates}

Class-specific trimming rates for the Italian Olive Oil and Statlog Shuttle
analyses are provided in \texttt{class\_trimming\_rates.csv} under
\texttt{results/real\_data\_results/}. These rates
document how Stage-I trimming interacts with external reference classes.

\section*{S10. Real-Data Microcluster Compositions}

Accepted residual micro-cluster compositions are provided in
\texttt{olive\_microcluster\_composition.csv} and
\texttt{shuttle\_microcluster\_composition.csv} under
\texttt{results/real\_data\_results/}.
The Shuttle outputs show pure local Bypass-dominated micro-clusters but also
fragmentation of the external Bypass class, consistent with the negative
residual local ARI reported in the main text.
